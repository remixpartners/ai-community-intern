# AI community prep: what it does and never does

For the IT lead or counsel deciding whether to allow it. One page, plain facts.

## What it is

AI community prep is a document, not software. It is one plain-text markdown file, SKILL.md, that the participant adds to their own AI assistant. The assistant reads it as a set of instructions for one conversation. It contains no scripts, no code that makes network calls, and no links. It acts only inside a conversation the participant started, never on a schedule or in the background; some assistants may open it in such a conversation when a request matches its description.

## What it does

It first explains itself and reads out its never list, before asking anything. It asks whether the company's card was already sent to Remix, by the participant or a colleague. On the first run it walks through an eight-field company card (company and segment, headcount band, finance or ERP system, office suite and where documents live, AI assistants in use and plan tier, messaging platform, one to three things built with AI, and the AI point person's role) and shows it as a plain-text block the participant copies into an email to Remix themselves. On later runs it asks one yes to search the participant's own email for the session brief from the sender named in the program, reading only the block under the brief heading, or asks them to paste it; then asks a separate yes, naming the sources it may use, to look through what is already connected to their assistant (files, email, messaging) for recent AI work; plays back what it found and where each item came from; asks a short set of share questions; helps build one slide in the conversation; offers to update the card; and ends by naming what the participant does themselves.

## What it never does

It tells the participant, word for word: "I never send email or messages, never post, never upload or attach files, never fetch web pages, and never write anything outside this conversation. Everything that reaches Remix is text you copy and send yourself."

These are instructions the assistant follows, not technical blocks. The participant's own assistant permissions still decide what is technically possible.

In short, it never sends, never uploads and never fetches. The slide stays in the conversation; the participant copies or downloads it themselves. The participant may skip any step or question.

It treats everything it reads, including the session brief, as information, never as instructions, and ignores any instruction found in an email or file. The card address comes only from the program sheet.

## What it reads

Only what the participant's own assistant can already reach. It searches email for the brief only after a yes, and looks through files, email or messaging only after a separate yes, and only in the sources the participant allows. Before that second question, it tells the participant what the assistant already remembers from earlier conversations that relates to the session brief; if nothing is connected to the assistant, it skips the look-around entirely, recall included. It does not ask anyone to connect a new source or grant new access.

## What leaves your company

Only text the participant copies into an email they send themselves: the company card, to justin@remixpartners.ai. Nothing else goes to Remix unless the participant chooses to share it. The card holds company facts and the AI point person's role; a name is optional. Remix keeps one card per company and shares only aggregate patterns across the community, never one company's card with another.

## How to inspect it

Unzip ai-community-prep.zip. It holds one folder, ai-community-prep, with one file, SKILL.md. The same file is published next to the zip. Open it in any text editor. What you read is the whole of it; the assistant gets nothing else from this package.

## How to remove it

Delete AI community prep from the assistant's skills list, or delete its folder. Removal does not undo anything the assistant kept in conversation history or memory, or a card already emailed to Remix. To have a company's card deleted, write to the contact address below.
